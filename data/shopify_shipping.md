# General Store & Fulfillment Guidelines

## Shipping & Delivery Policies
* Orders are processed within 24 hours on business days (Monday through Friday).
* Flat-rate standard shipping costs $4.99 per order, while orders over $50 qualify for free standard shipping.
* International delivery options are available at checkout and usually take between 7 to 14 business days depending on customs processing.

## Order Cancellations
* Orders can be canceled or edited within 1 hour of placement before they enter the fulfillment phase.
* To request an immediate cancellation, customers can use the self-serve order status page or contact support directly.

## Refund Rules & Exceptions
* Perishable goods, personal care items, and clearance final-sale items are not eligible for standard returns.
* Store credits and direct product exchanges are processed immediately once the returned item is inspected at our warehouse.
